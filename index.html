Reproducible method

A leakage-safe path from rows to decisions.

The original assignment's model family is preserved, while preprocessing order is corrected so test information cannot influence training.

01

Clean

Keep one combined TOT row for traded players and repair undefined shooting percentages.

02

Split

Reserve a stratified 25% test set before learning any preprocessing parameters.

03

Transform

Fit mean imputation and standardization on training rows, then transform the test rows.

04

Balance

Use SMOTE only on transformed training data to bring each position to 88 examples.

05

Select

Use five-fold RFECV with balanced accuracy to retain 12 of 26 numeric features.

06

Classify

Tune a linear SVM with stratified folds, then evaluate once on the untouched test set.

Research foundation

The page is tied to the methods it uses.

Primary papers provide the basis for synthetic oversampling, maximum-margin classification, and recursive feature elimination.

Chawla et al. · 2002

SMOTE: Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique

Generates minority-class examples by interpolating between neighboring observations.

Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research ↗ Cortes & Vapnik · 1995

Support-Vector Networks

Introduces the soft-margin support vector approach used for non-separable classification.

Machine Learning ↗ Guyon et al. · 2002

Gene Selection Using Support Vector Machines

Establishes recursive feature elimination as a way to retain a compact discriminative feature set.
